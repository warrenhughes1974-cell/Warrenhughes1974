5. Loan Calculations
• Problem: Discrepancies in loan calculations due to a misunderstanding of Life Pro's field definitions, particularly regarding "loan amount added" (which seems to be interest in advance) and "loan balance."
• The data cut being used is from 07/31, while the local data might be from 06/30, causing mismatches.
• Life Pro Logic: Life Pro includes the current year's interest in the surrender amount, then backs out the interest to arrive at the true loan balance.
• Example: Loan amount $3506.80 includes accrued interest of $8.88.
• The "in-advance balance" is the starting balance, not the true loan balance.
• "Negative accrued interest" in the table further complicates understanding.
• Goal: Correctly calculate the loan balance by accounting for interest in advance and other components.
• Calculation Method:
• Life Pro provides the loan balance at the next anniversary (e.g., 07/24/2027 value).
• To get the prior year's balance (e.g., 07/24/2026 value), the interest needs to be backed out.
• The calculation involves multiplying the current loan balance by $(1 - \text{interest rate})$.
• The interest rate is policy-specific (e.g., 5%, 7.4%). $$ \text{Prior Year Loan Balance} = \text{Current Loan Balance} \times (1 - \text{Interest Rate}) $$
• Action: Need to obtain 07/31 data to match the provided examples. The loan balance will be calculated by reverse-engineering the interest based on the policy's anniversary and specific interest rate. This will resolve the issue for future loans as well. This will be added to the issues log.
