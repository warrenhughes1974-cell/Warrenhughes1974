## Field Definitions

- **Decision ID** — Unique identifier (e.g. `CIT-DEC-001`)
- **Approval Status** — `PROPOSED` | `APPROVED` | `REJECTED` | `SUPERSEDED`
- **Notes** — Evidence, risks, links

## Update Instructions

1. Change `PROPOSED` → `APPROVED` only with explicit human sign-off in chat or written approval.
2. Never delete historical rows; supersede with a new decision ID when needed.
3. Stage 4A seeded CIT-DEC-001…020 as **PROPOSED** only (2026-07-12).
